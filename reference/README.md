## Attribution within the technical report

The report is a six-person team document. My own written contributions were the
**Project Management** section in full, the **Aerodynamics** section in full,
and **Design and Manufacturing 3.1–3.2** (downselection and subassembly design).
The Structures sections and Design and Manufacturing 3.3–3.5 were written by
teammates. See [`docs/my-contribution.md`](../docs/my-contribution.md).

## A note on what is not here

The individual reflective portfolio submitted alongside this report is not
published. It contains personal identifiers, third-party performance
information, and interpersonal detail about teammates who did not consent to
publication. The professionally relevant content from it — roles held, tasks
owned, and what I would do differently — is written up in
[`docs/my-contribution.md`](../docs/my-contribution.md) and
[`docs/09-lessons-learned.md`](../docs/09-lessons-learned.md) instead.

Teaching material reproduced in the technical report under academic fair dealing
has also been excluded from the figure set.
